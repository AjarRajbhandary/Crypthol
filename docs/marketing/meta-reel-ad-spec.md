## 1. Ad Strategy Brief

### What Pondros is
Pondros tracks action items and follow-through inside Slack. Its thesis: *follow-through is the real work of management, and almost nobody instruments it.* The product loop is **Capture → Chase → Complete**.

### Target persona
**"The Human Reminder."** Founder, agency owner, ops lead or account director at a **10–50 person, client-facing, Slack-heavy team**: agencies, consultancies, ops teams and small service businesses.

- The team runs on Slack. They have tried Asana, ClickUp or a PM tool, but "nobody opens them."
- They are the one who remembers what everyone promised, and they spend part of every day chasing people.
- **Not** enterprise IT buying a 4,000-seat PPM suite, and not students.

### Core pain point
**Commitments made in Slack slip.** Action items get agreed in a thread or after a client call ("on it 👍"). Then the conversation scrolls away, nobody checks whether the work happened, and the manager finds out *when the client asks.*

The pain is **follow-through**. It is not note-taking and it is not generic software shopping.

### Primary hook angle
**Problem → Agitate → Solve, opened with a relatable "On it 👍" pattern interrupt.**
- **Problem:** A familiar Slack moment that everyone recognises.
- **Agitate:** The client chase, and the identity sting: "You're not a manager, you're a human reminder."
- **Solve:** Pondros captures the commitment and chases it inside Slack, with no new tool to open.

### Core value proposition
> **Pondros turns what your team promises in Slack into tracked commitments, and follows up for you, so nothing falls through the cracks and you stop being the human reminder.**

Supporting proof points:
1. **Lives in Slack.** No new tool the team won't open.
2. **Captures commitments** with an owner and a due date. **[VERIFY: auto-capture vs. manual]**
3. **Chases for you.** It nudges the owner, not you. **[VERIFY nudge mechanics]**
4. **Shows you what's open vs. done** at a glance, which is useful for a weekly review. **[VERIFY]**

Positioning vs. alternatives:
- **Heavy PM tools (Asana, ClickUp):** Pondros meets the team where they already work.
- **Slackbot reminders, Chaser and generic bots:** Pondros is built around commitments and accountability, not one-off reminders.

## 4. Production Notes

### Audio / music
- **VO-led.** The voiceover drives the story. Use a natural, slightly dry founder or peer voice, not an announcer. Record on a phone or lav in a treated room so it feels like UGC.
- **Bed:** a low-tempo lo-fi or minimal "office-tech" beat at −18 to −22 dB under the VO. A drop or snap at 0:12 (the Solve reveal) and a satisfying "tick" SFX on each ✅.
- **SFX that add realism:** a Slack-style knock/notification (use an original or licensed sound, not Slack's), a message-send whoosh and a phone buzz at the client-message moment.
- If you test a **trending audio** variant, use it only from Meta's commercial-use sound library for a business account, and keep the VO on top.
- **Design for sound-off.** The overlays alone must tell the whole story.

### Caption styling
- **Burned-in captions:** 2–6 words per card, bold geometric sans (e.g. Inter Bold / SF Pro Display Heavy) at 64–80px on a 1080-wide canvas. White text on a semi-opaque dark pill, or black on the brand accent colour. Keep contrast at 4.5:1 or better.
- **One idea per card.** Animate in with a quick pop or scale of 3–5 frames and hold each card for at least 1.2s.
- Highlight the key word in the brand accent colour: "**human reminder**", "**tracked**", "**chases**".
- Also run full auto-captions or a word-by-word VO subtitle track in a lighter weight, positioned above the headline overlays or in the same band.

### Placement: Reels safe zones (1080×1920)
- **Top ~14% (≈270px):** keep clear. The account name and the "Reels" header sit here.
- **Bottom ~35% (≈670px):** keep clear of text and key UI. The caption, CTA button and audio ticker sit here.
- **Right edge ~120px:** keep clear. The like, comment, share and ⋯ buttons sit here.
- **Left edge:** leave at least 64px of margin.
- **Put all overlays and key UI in the band from roughly y = 300 to 1250px,** shifted slightly left of centre. Scale screen recordings so the important card (owner / due / Done) lands inside this band.
- Check the edit in Meta's Ads Manager placement preview before publishing.

### Screen recording and brand
- Use real Pondros UI in a clean demo workspace with realistic names and a fictional client ("Acme"). Zoom to 150–200% so the UI is readable on a phone. Add cursor-tap highlights.
- **Slack trademark:** show Slack as the context where Pondros works, but do not use Slack's logo as a hero element or imply that Slack endorses Pondros. Use a lightly stylised chat UI or genuine in-product captures, following Slack's partner and brand guidelines. **[VERIFY with legal/brand]**
- Keep it honest. Show no invented metrics, testimonials or customer logos unless they are real and approved. This also lowers Meta ad-review risk.

### Test plan for the launch
- **1 campaign / 1 ad set (Advantage+ audience or broad):** ages 25–55, owners, founders and ops roles, plus interests in Slack, agencies and project management. Placement: Reels + Stories.
- **Ads:** 4 hook variants (H1–H4) × primary text A/B/C. Let Advantage+ creative rotate them, then kill hooks whose 3-second view rate falls below the account median after about 2–3k impressions.
- **KPIs to watch:**
  - Hook rate (3-second views ÷ impressions): 30% or higher is a target.
  - Hold rate (ThruPlays ÷ 3-second views).
  - CTR (link).
  - Cost per sign-up.
